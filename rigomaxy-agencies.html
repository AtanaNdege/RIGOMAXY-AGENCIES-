Who We Are

Built on Trust,
Driven by Excellence

Rigomaxy Agencies is a Kisumu-based automotive services company dedicated to delivering dependable, high-quality car solutions for individuals, families, and businesses. We combine professionalism with a personal touch — because every journey matters.

From a quick city drop-off to a long-term vehicle hire, our team is equipped to handle all your mobility needs with speed, safety, and a smile.

Our Vision

Our Values
